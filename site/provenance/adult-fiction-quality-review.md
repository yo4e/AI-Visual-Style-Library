# Adult editorial sample review

Five reference-free samples were generated from the English instructions frozen on 2026-10-11. Two additional trials used the identical reportage and ballpoint prompts; they did not improve sparseness enough to displace the first outputs. Selected source and final WebP hashes are in the benchmark manifest and actual-submission records. Generation prompts were not rewritten to match outputs.

All selected images retain an adult woman, coat, backpack, two-handed open map, outdoor platform, canopy, white train, and teal train stripe. They are drawn illustrations with restrained adult expressions, not photographic portraits. No supplied cover images, original characters, typography, or cover layouts were used as references.

| Treatment | Visible result | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reportage ink | Fine dry contours, dark hair accents, restrained rust color, pale paper-like surfaces | Architecture and map are more detailed, and color occupies more space, than the sparse instruction requests. |
| Loose contour / flat color | Visible brown hand-drawn edges, broad matte color planes, mature figure | Lines are more controlled and background shadows stronger than the deliberately loose, minimally shaded request. |
| Oil pastel | Broad broken waxy-looking pigment, warm/cool stroke masses, smaller facial marks | Small map and finger details remain schematic; the scene has more texture than a very minimal illustration. |
| Blue ballpoint | Blue overlapping pen marks, selected hatching, warm clothing accent, pale open surfaces | Background line density and accent area exceed the sparse request. |
| Stipple duotone | Navy dot clusters, rust planes, paper highlights, visibly drawn features | Dots extend across background surfaces; dark hair dots merge at thumbnail size. |

The techniques are meaningfully distinguishable by mark, paint, and palette, while faces and scene construction can overlap. Airy is the prompt's intended density choice; the two selected airy samples still have detailed architecture. Bilingual sample notes expose these differences. One selected realization and one moderate retry for two prompts do not establish alternate-subject, repeated-generation, or cross-model reproducibility.
