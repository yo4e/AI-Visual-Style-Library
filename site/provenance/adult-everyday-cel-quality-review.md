# Adult everyday cel-animation review

The user-uploaded reference was obtained through Library materialization and its actual pixels were inspected locally. The original reference stays outside the repository and public build. It was used to analyze rendering rather than as an image-generation input.

Observed generic traits: naturally proportioned adults, modest almond eyes, clearly drawn noses and mouths, fine understated charcoal outlines, broad hair shapes, restrained hard-edged cel shadows, subdued clothing colors, and observed architectural backgrounds with an everyday atmosphere. The source's characters, clothing, animals, books, poster panels and typography are not reproduced. Public titles, descriptions and English generation prompts contain no author or work names.

English style and scene instructions were saved before the single reference-free generation call and submitted unchanged. The selected sample retains the common adult woman, coat, backpack, two-handed open map, platform, canopy, distant commuters and white train with teal stripe.

Visible correspondence: thin character contours, broad hair shapes with selective separation lines, flat skin with a discrete neck shadow, economical clothing folds and hard shadow planes, muted local colors, and legible everyday station architecture. The result is a drawn 2D animation illustration rather than a photographic portrait or boldly deformed icon.

Visible differences: the selected output's eyes are rounder and its far background edges softer than those in the reference. It also uses some subtle gradients beyond the requested mostly flat cel treatment. These limitations appear in the bilingual sample notes; generation instructions were not rewritten to match the output.

This is one selected realization, not a promise of exact reference reproduction or repeated, alternate-subject, or cross-model reproducibility. All previous 74 styles and their assets remain intact.
